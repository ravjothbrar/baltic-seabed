# Who else to send Baltic Seabed Watch to

Ranked by how directly the build speaks to what each company does. Find **named people**, never a careers inbox;
check names and titles on LinkedIn before sending. Sequence and message templates are in `OUTREACH.md`.

## Tier 1: the build is almost their product
| Company | Why it fits | Angle for the first line |
|---|---|---|
| **Helsing** (Lura / SG-1 Fathom) | One operator tasking hundreds of gliders for undersea surveillance | "I built what that operator's screen might look like" |
| **Stark Defence** (DE) | Announced the Vanta 24 uncrewed vessel to protect cables and watch the shadow fleet in the Baltic and North Sea, with software that lets drones and vessels share data | "Here's the shared picture your Vanta fleet would feed" |
| **Windward** | Maritime AI for dark activity and sanctions risk; the same fusion problem, commercially | "Behaviour signals with explicit innocent explanations" |
| **ICEYE** (FI) | SAR satellites detect ships that switch AIS off; Finnish, so the Gulf of Finland is home turf | "Where your radar detections would close my DARK gaps" |
| **Unseenlabs** (FR) | RF satellites detect non-cooperating ships | Same as ICEYE, RF instead of radar |
| **Euroatlas** (DE) | GreyShark underwater patrol drones being tested in the Baltic | Glider/UUV coverage layer |
| **SUBmerge Baltic** (LV) | Underwater drones for cable inspection, scaling production | Cable-corridor prioritisation for their fleet |
| **Optics11** (NL) | Turns cables into passive listening sensors | "Which corridors most need a listening curtain" |

## Tier 2: partners, asset owners, and the people who'd buy it
- **Ocean Infinity, QinetiQ, Blue Ocean Marine Tech Systems**: named Lura partners.
- **Fingrid, Elering, Cinia, Elisa, Arelion**: owners of the cables in the incident log. Elisa runs distributed acoustic sensing on its cables.
- **NATO Baltic Sentry / NATO DIANA**: the operation and the defence-innovation accelerator.
- **Saildrone**: uncrewed surface vessels deployed with northern European navies.

## Tier 3: large companies (need a specific route in)
- **Palantir**: big company, but it runs the **Meritocracy Fellowship**, a paid four-month programme in New York for
  high-school graduates not enrolled in college, with some fellows offered full-time roles. Check eligibility
  for UK applicants (visa/work authorisation) on the official posting. The build is a strong
  "show, don't tell" attachment. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/11/05/palantir-colleges-no-longer-reliable-training-ground-gen-z-hire-22-high-school-students-fellowship/), [Pirate Wires](https://piratewires.com/p/inside-palantirs-bet-on-high-school-grads))
- **Anduril** (UK office; underwater autonomy), **Saab**, **Kongsberg**, **Thales UK**, **BAE Systems**: apply through
  early-careers routes and mention the link; cold builds rarely reach a decision-maker at this size.

DARK signal and its coverage caveat. For drone makers, lead with the glider layer. For asset owners, lead with
their cable's corridor count.
